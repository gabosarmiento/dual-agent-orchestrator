Connecting

Project

This app mirrors existing tmux panes. To work from your Mac terminal and the browser interchangeably, launch Claude and Codex inside separate tmux sessions, then attach each pane here. Existing sessions launched outside tmux cannot be adopted retroactively.

Connect your accounts

Choose a project first, then create managed sessions. Click Connect to open the official login flow in that session. Your credentials stay local; no environment variables or terminal commands to copy.

Login commands are issued inside isolated, app-created terminal sessions. When prompted, follow the official provider sign-in steps shown in your terminal panel. Existing unmanaged sessions remain available for terminal mirroring and handoffs; the app will not overwrite their authentication.

Agent sessions

Shared handoff

The app keeps both sessions running. Sending a handoff pastes context into the selected agent and presses Enter. Verify the agent is ready to accept a new prompt first; sending into a busy terminal may interrupt its work.
